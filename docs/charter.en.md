# Charter

English | [中文](charter.md)

## Vision

ZTask is a team progress management tool: it gives a team one consistent answer to "where does this goal stand right now, who owns it, and what is next". It serves progress visibility, not process control.

## Boundaries

- In scope: progress modeling (goals, milestones, item status), progress presentation, status consistency under multi-person collaboration.
- Out of scope (initial stage): full project management (Gantt charts, resource scheduling), instant messaging, fine-grained permission systems.
- Boundaries evolve with stages; shrinking or expanding them means editing this file, never a verbal agreement.

## Current state

The project now has a runnable CLI, server, admin shell, shared contract, and storage-recovery foundation. Progress modeling and task workflows continue through M3 and later milestones. The documentation system and development-flow gates are initialized and maintained with implementation.

The roadmap and current milestones live in [.agents/flow/roadmap.md](../.agents/flow/roadmap.en.md). Expanding product boundaries starts in this file, followed by adjustment of deliverables in the flow tree.
